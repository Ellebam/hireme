T-024 Design Direction

Design Prototypes

Five distinct design directions for HireMe. Each prototype includes dashboard, editor, templates, and export modal — fully interactive. Click to explore.

Noir Luxe
Design 01
Noir Luxe
Dark luxury with gold accents. Playfair Display serif headings, deep charcoal surfaces, subtle gold shimmer effects. Sophisticated and premium — like a high-end portfolio.
Dark Mode Serif Gold Accents Luxury
Glass Garden
Design 02
Glass Garden
Glassmorphism with organic floating shapes. Animated gradient blobs, frosted glass panels, soft pastels. Ethereal and dreamy — like a modern art gallery meets productivity tool.
Glassmorphism Animated BG Pastels Organic
Paper Craft
Design 03
Paper Craft
Warm, tactile, artisanal feel. Cream textures, Fraunces serif, burnt sienna accents, offset box shadows. Like beautifully designed stationery — approachable and distinctive.
Warm Tones Textured Monospace Artisanal
Chromatic Wave
Design 04
Chromatic Wave
Bold gradients, dynamic shapes, bouncy animations. Purple-pink-cyan color shifts, wavy hero banner, springy micro-interactions. Energetic and vibrant — Spotify meets Notion.
Gradients Bouncy Dark Hero Energetic
Editorial
Design 05
Editorial
Magazine-grade typography, dramatic whitespace, monochrome with vermillion accent. Newsreader serif, sharp borders, no rounded corners. Publication-quality — Bloomberg meets CV builder.
Typography-First Monochrome Sharp Edges Editorial
